# Tutoring workflow

This repository contains quant interview study notes and progress, not general personal memory.

Before tutoring, read MEMORY.md, the curriculum index and state/progress.json. Use the user's requested chapter if specified. Use English question stems and Chinese explanations, following the learner's latest request (2026-10-28), with explicit problem inputs, outputs, assumptions, reasoning, derivation and boundary checks. Explain the bridge from a familiar problem to its variant.

Keep source access, solution verification and learner mastery as separate fields. Never infer mastery from a topic having been explained. Do not claim a private/paywalled original was read when only a public equivalent was inspected.

Preserve corrections and source URLs. Do not add copyrighted full PDFs, scraped full solution corpora, credentials, unrelated personal history, or unverified company-question provenance. Only original explanations, short metadata and source pointers belong here.


For every counting problem, identify the counted objects, whether order matters, whether repetition is allowed, and why each factor uses combinations, permutations, powers or the multiplication principle. A single problem may use different tools in different steps. Explain alternative methods where they clarify the counting.

Follow the learner's mainline strictly: combinatorial probability, conditional probability and Bayes, distributions, expectation, then optimal stopping. Keep each batch thematically coherent. Inspect actual question content and prerequisites; do not follow a broad website tag or alphabetical family ordering blindly. Defer standalone number-theory proofs and binary-search algorithms to their later chapters. When replacing an unanswered batch, retain its numbers, clearly supersede the old prompts, and do not increase attempted or mastered counts.

Current active workflow (2026-10-29): use LEARNING_PLAN.md, data/learning_units.json and state/microcourse_progress.json. Teach one coherent micro-unit at a time: theory, fully worked Train 1, guided Train 2, independent practice Train 3 with feedback, then two fresh Test items without hints before the first learner submission. Earlier exposed questions and near-number-only variants belong to Train. Full-stem and answer validation are required before a title-routed test candidate is issued. Keep first test attempts, corrected practice and historical exposure separate; do not resume the old fixed-ten queue automatically.
